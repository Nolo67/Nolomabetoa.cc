About Me

I am a Data Analyst specializing in marketing operations, business intelligence, and process automation. I hold a Master of Science in Business Analytics from Hult International Business School (San Francisco) and a BCom in Economics & Econometrics from the University of Johannesburg.

My work focuses on converting complex datasets into clear, automated reporting systems and actionable business intelligence. Leveraging Python, SQL, Power BI, Tableau, and Scikit-learn, I specialize in campaign performance analytics, ETL process automation, and predictive modeling—including customer churn modeling and executive KPI dashboards. With a background spanning advertising ops, economics, and advanced data analytics, I bridge the gap between technical data engineering and executive decision-making.
